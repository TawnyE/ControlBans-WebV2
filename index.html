Moderation that actually works

ControlBans is a complete punishment and moderation system for Minecraft networks. Bans, mutes, warnings, auto-escalation, cross-server sync, and a built-in GUI — all in one plugin.

Everything you need to moderate

From a quick ban to a full multi-server punishment network, ControlBans has you covered.

Full Punishment Suite

Bans, tempbans, mutes, shadow mutes, warnings, kicks, IP bans, voice mutes, freeze, void jail, and skin bans. 20+ commands out of the box.

Auto-Escalation

Define templates with #hashtags. Repeat offenders automatically get harsher punishments based on their history. 34 built-in templates included.

Cross-Server Sync

Built-in Velocity and BungeeCord support. Punish a player on one server and it applies across your entire network instantly.

Punishment GUI

Staff can open a clickable GUI with /punish to apply common punishments without typing commands. No training required.

Multi-Database

SQLite, MySQL, MariaDB, PostgreSQL, H2, and MongoDB. Start with SQLite and switch to MySQL later without losing any data.

10 Built-in Locales

English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Dutch, Portuguese, Russian, and Turkish. Every message is customizable.
