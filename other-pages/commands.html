Commands

Every command ControlBans adds. Most commands accept a -s flag before the player name to issue the punishment silently.

Bans

CommandDescriptionPermission
/ban [-s] <player> [reason]Permanently ban a player.controlbans.ban
/tempban [-s] <player> <time> [reason]Temporarily ban a player.controlbans.tempban
/unban [-s] <player|ip>Unban a player or IP.controlbans.unban
/ipban [-s] <player|ip> <time> [reason]Ban all accounts sharing an IP.controlbans.ban.ip
/unipban <ip>Unban an IP address.controlbans.unipban
/banlist [page]View all active bans.controlbans.banlist

Silent Flag

Most commands accept -s to issue the punishment silently. Only staff with controlbans.notify.silent see silent punishments.

# Normal ban (broadcast to everyone)
/ban Connor cheating

# Silent ban (only staff see it)
/ban -s Connor cheating
